The first week

Arriving and settling in

Two things about that day. It is hot — I came from Dezhou, and Hainan in August is a different kind of heat. And the campus is not beside the station: the high-speed stop is Baimajing, about 15 km away, so the last leg is a taxi you arrange rather than improvise.

Important: the documents you need and the registration process can change from year to year. The offer letter and the university's own notices are what count. This page is about the order and the experience, not the official rules.

Do these in this order

  1. Walk round the campus before you queue for anything

    This is what I did first, and it turned out to be the useful part. Ten minutes of walking tells you which building is which, instead of asking someone every day for a week.

  2. Collect your new-student pack

    One place, one queue, and it is the longest queue of the day. Have the documents below in your hand before you join it.

  3. At the dorm: register your face, then take the luggage up

    Door access is set up at the block itself. Do that first, or you will be standing in the sun with a suitcase waiting for somebody to let you in.

  4. Clean the room and meet the people next door

    Nobody schedules this part, but it is the part that decides how the first month feels.

Documents to bring

What you will be asked for on registration day
ItemNotes
Offer letterOriginal. This is the first thing they ask for.
ID cardOriginal, plus a copy for yourself
Household register page (户口本单页)Only if you are moving your household registration to Hainan. You cannot arrange this after you have arrived.
One-inch photosThey go on your student card, so bring more than one.

What you need on the first night

I bought almost nothing on the first night. A bin, and the things you cannot get through one night without, from the supermarket on campus. The rest waited.

  • A bin for the room
  • Toiletries and slippers
  • A power strip

Easy things to forget

  • Order the odd things before you fly. There is no big shopping centre near the campus, and a parcel to the island takes about five days.
  • If you are moving your household registration, that single page from your household register has to travel in your luggage — it cannot be sorted out from here afterwards.
  • Bring more one-inch photos than you think you need. They go on your student card.
