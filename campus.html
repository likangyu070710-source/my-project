Once you have moved in

Campus and nearby

Most days my walk is short: down from the dorm, across to the first floor of the Student Center for a meal, then over to the teaching building. The parcel lockers are in my own block, so even the errands do not take me far. Use the buttons to narrow the list.

Everyday places

Showing all places

Student canteen

Where: first floor of the Student Center

Cost: about 15 yuan a meal — cheap, and about what you would expect a canteen to be

Advice: this is where you will eat most days. It fills up the moment classes end, so go early or accept the queue.

Flavour restaurant

Where: the other half of the same floor

What: the 风味餐厅. Lanzhou beef noodles, braised pork trotter rice, cured meat rice — and a Western counter that is not very good.

Advice: come to this side when the ordinary canteen starts to taste the same every day. Same floor, so look at both before you queue.

Meiyijia (美宜佳)

Where: the supermarket on campus

Good for: a bin, water, tissues — the things you need on day one

Advice: some items cost a little more than outside. For anything you will use up over a term, order it online instead.

Parcel lockers

Where: in the dorm buildings, on the ground floor of your own block

How: the courier sends a pickup code to your phone. Type the code into the locker, the door opens, take your parcel. No desk, no queue.

Advice: collect it the day it arrives. A pickup code does not stay valid forever, and a locker is not a cupboard.

Shared printers

Where: there is no print shop on campus — there are shared printers

How: connect to the campus network and print from there. The university gives you 50 yuan of printing to start with.

Advice: set it up once in your first week, not the night before something is due.

Getting into town

How far: about 40 km. The centre of Danzhou is Nada (那大镇).

How: a taxi costs more than 50 yuan. There is also a bus for 2 yuan, but it only runs in the morning and the evening.

Advice: the bus keeps to fixed times and is not convenient. Check them before you plan a whole day around it.

Xingfu Cheng (幸福城)

Where: about 3 km from campus — the nearest place that has food

What: a night market and a row of restaurants. Around 8 pm is when it is busiest.

How: a shared bike, about ten minutes. Nothing much to eat just outside the gate, so this is the trip.

Written by Kangyu Li, a current BiUH student · 2026
